Fuel tanks are welded, baffled and tested in-house. Each compartment has its own filler, vent and drain, and the shell is pressure-tested before it is painted and signed.

The finished unit carries earthing points front and rear, a lockable cabinet and the hazard markings required for petroleum transport.
