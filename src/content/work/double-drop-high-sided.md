A double-drop body carries more volume without raising the loading height. We build the side profile in-house, with a heavier bottom section and a reinforcing rib at the drop so the panels stay flat under a full load.

The sides are hinged and removable, which lets the same truck run bulk grain one week and bagged cargo the next.
