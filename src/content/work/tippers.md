The tipper is the body most Kenyan fleets live on, so this one was built to take punishment: a 5 mm wear-resistant floor, reinforced sides and a single front-end ram that tips cleanly even when the load is wet.

We fabricate the body, fit the hoist and hydraulic tank, then paint it in the customer's fleet colours. It leaves Athi River ready to work, with the hoist tested under load before handover.
