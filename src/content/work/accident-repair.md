Accident repair is the other half of what we do. Cabs are stripped, pulled and straightened on the frame, panels are beaten or replaced, and the whole repair is primed, blocked and painted to a matched finish.

We work with insurance assessors and direct customers, and photograph each stage so the owner can see what was done under the paint.
