A recovery body has to carry its own weight and then lift somebody else's. We build the deck and boom mounts around the chassis outriggers so the load goes into the frame, not the body floor.

Lockers along both flanks take chains, straps and the wheel-lift gear, and the beacon bar is wired through a protected loom.
