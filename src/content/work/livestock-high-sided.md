Livestock work is unforgiving: the body has to ventilate, drain and wash out without rusting through. These two units were built with slatted upper panels, a sealed floor and internal gates that split the load into pens.

The ramp is non-slip and folds against the tail, and every hinge and latch is galvanised so it keeps working after a washdown.
