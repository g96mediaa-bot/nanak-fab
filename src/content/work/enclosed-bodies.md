For dry goods and distribution work we build a light, weather-tight box on a galvanised steel frame. The aluminium skin keeps the tare weight down; the frame takes the knocks that come with daily loading.

This body was specified with a rear roller shutter, a side access door and full-length tie rails so the customer could run pallets and loose cartons on the same truck.
