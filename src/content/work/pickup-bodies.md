A pickup body is a small job that gets used every day, so we build it like a truck body rather than a trailer: a braced steel frame under a 3 mm checker-plate deck, with sides that fold flat.

This one carries a ladder rack, two lockable tool boxes under the deck and a headboard rated for a full load of bagged stock.
