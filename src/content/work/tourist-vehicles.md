Coach bodies are built over a jig so the cage stays square: floor frame, side pillars, roof bows and the front and rear bulkheads, all welded and braced before any skin goes on.

Glazing, trim, luggage bays and paint are finished in-house, so the operator deals with one workshop from bare chassis to finished coach.
