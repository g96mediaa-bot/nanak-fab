The hydraulic exhauster is the heavier-duty version: a PTO-driven pump and a larger tank, built for municipal crews that empty pits and septic tanks all day.

We mount the pump and tank to keep the road weight inside the chassis rating, and run the discharge controls to the rear so the operator never has to climb on the body.
